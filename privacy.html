Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-09-29

Miles Mail is a personal application operated by its owner for their own use. It accesses exactly one email account: its operator's own.

  • What it accesses: the application reads and sends email in its operator's own Google mailbox, with the account owner's authorization.
  • What it collects from others: nothing. The application has no third-party users, no sign-ups, no tracking, no analytics, and no advertising.
  • Data sharing and sale: none. Mailbox data is not shared with, transferred to, or sold to any third party.
  • Storage: mail remains in the Google account. Limited working data stays on the operator's own private equipment.
  • Revocation: the account owner can revoke the application's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Contact: the operating account's own address.

Miles Mail's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
