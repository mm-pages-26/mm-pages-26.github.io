Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-09-27

Miles Mail is a private application operated by a single household for its own use. It accesses exactly one email account: its own.

  • What it accesses: the application reads and sends email in its own Google mailbox, with the account owner's authorization, to provide personal-assistant functions to the household that operates it.
  • What it collects from others: nothing. The application has no third-party users, no sign-ups, no tracking, no analytics, and no advertising.
  • Data sharing and sale: none. Mailbox data is not shared with, transferred to, or sold to any third party, and is not used for any purpose beyond the household's own assistant functions.
  • Storage: mail remains in the Google account. Limited working data (message identifiers, short excerpts) is kept on the household's own private server.
  • Revocation: the account owner can revoke the application's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Contact: the account's own address — this application and its mailbox share an owner.

Miles Mail's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
